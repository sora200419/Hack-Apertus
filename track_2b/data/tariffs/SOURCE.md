# PRC import tariff rates (`cn_import_tariffs.json`)

Generated by `python -m originpass.tariffs_build` from `data/raw/cn_tariff_ch_fta_2014_gacc_53.tsv`,
the official schedule published with **GACC Announcement 2014 No. 53, Annex 1**
(《中国-瑞士自由贸易协定》2014年协定税率表: 8,277 PRC national tariff lines with the 2014 MFN rate and the
Switzerland conventional rate). The annex text was taken from the customs.gov.cn mirror in
github.com/Pinky-Lemon6/customs_spider (`dataset/data/regulations.json`, record 海关总署公告2014年第53号).

- Per HS6 subheading, the national line with the **smallest MFN–FTA margin** is used, so the duty saving
  shown is a lower bound; the other lines under the subheading are listed in each entry's `source`.
- These are the rates **at entry into force (1 July 2014)**. The FTA phase-down and later MFN cuts make
  current rates lower; check the current PRC tariff before quoting a saving to a customer.
- The upgraded FTA (concluded 20 Aug 2026) will change the conventional rates again (e.g. 100 % duty-free
  coverage for HS 91 and HS 84 per the SECO fact sheet); not yet published, not encoded.
